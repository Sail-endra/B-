AI privacy and explanations

Voice learning · ElevenLabs

With your permission, your recording is sent to ElevenLabs for transcription. When you choose Read aloud, the educational answer text is sent there for speech generation. Course Copilot does not keep the recording. ElevenLabs processes these requests under its own service terms; typed study remains available without voice.

Voice input is off. Activate the microphone button to record; it never listens in the background.

Voice Only Mode is on. The microphone listens only after you activate it.
